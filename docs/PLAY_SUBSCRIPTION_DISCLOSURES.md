# Google Play — subscription copy (paste into Console + keep in sync with app)

Use the same wording in **Play Console → Monetize → Subscriptions** and in the app (Settings / Strategy Lab paywall).

## Astronaut (`lottopilot_astronaut_monthly`)

**Title:** Astronaut Plan — Strategy Lab

**Description:**

- Unlimited Strategy Lab (Generate, Refine, Auto Pilot)
- Ad-free in Strategy Lab only (Compass may still show ads unless Pirate Plan is purchased)
- 1-month free trial for eligible new subscribers, then **$0.99/month** (set your regional prices in Console)
- Renews automatically until canceled in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions

**Free trial offer:** New customer acquisition only (Google enforces — reinstalling the app does not reset eligibility on the same Google account).

## Pirate (`lottopilot_pirate`) — one-time

**Title:** Pirate Plan — Compass

**Description:**

- One-time purchase (not a subscription)
- Unlimited Compass pick generation, ad-free in Compass
- Typical price: **CA$3.99** (Canada) / **US$3.49** (United States) — configure regional prices in Play Console
- Does **not** include Astronaut / Strategy Lab

One-time in-app products are usually reviewed under general billing clarity, not the subscription trial policy — but a clear one-time label in Console still helps.
